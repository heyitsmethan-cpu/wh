What is Freecash?
It is a platform where users earn "coins" for completing tasks like testing new apps, playing mobile games, and taking surveys. These coins can then be cashed out for PayPal, Bitcoin, or Gift Cards.

How the Funnel Works

The beauty of the platform lies in its simplicity. You sign up, choose a "deal" (usually a mobile game), and complete the requirements. Most high-paying deals require you to reach a certain level in a game within a set timeframe.

  • Step 4: Claim your reward once the task is verified.
